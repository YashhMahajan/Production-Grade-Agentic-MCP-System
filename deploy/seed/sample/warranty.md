# Blender warranty

Every blender comes with a 24 month limited warranty. It covers the motor and the jar against defects in materials and workmanship.

The warranty does not cover damage from dropping the jar, running the motor with no liquid in it, or using the blender for anything other than food.

To make a claim, reply to your order confirmation with a photo of the serial number on the base. We replace the part or the whole blender within 10 business days.
